Program Details

About the Program

The GRMS MathCounts Program is a student-led team that participates at the MATHCOUNTS Diablo Chapter Competition, representing GRMS, every February. Our team has consistently beat other teams in recent years and have qualified for MATHCOUNTS State for the last two years.

Admissions

Selection Process

Our intensive selection test contains 30 short answer questions to be solved in 40 minutes. The top 15 students in the test directly qualify to our program, where they prepare weekly for the competition in February.

Results

Competition Results

Our team ranked 2nd and 3rd in recent years in the Tri Valley (Chapter) competition. In 2024 and 2025, we qualified for State and ranked 15th and 12th in State as a team. Furthermore, our students did extremely well these years, getting single digit ranks in Chapter and top 50 ranks in State.

Who are we?

About GR Math Counts

Gale Ranch Middle School Math Counts aims to provide interested math-oriented students with powerful guidance and competition experience. Our dedicated coaches make sure that a strong group of math students can use their skills in high-stakes, competitive situations.

Approximately a week after the selection test, students will be notified via email if they are accepted into the program or not. Who it's for

Anyone who is in middle school and attends Gale Ranch Middle School is welcome to try our selection test and register. However, we are looking for strong math individuals, so eighth graders will naturally fall more into our scope. This doesn't mean that higher grades get priority; if you score well as a sixth grader, you have an equally good chance of making the team.

Preparation

We strongly advise that students solve the practice problems given on the website as practice for the real selection test. In addition, MATHCOUNTS is similar to the AMC 8 and AMC 10 in difficulty. The competition consists of 30 short answer questions to be solved in 40 minutes. The questions slowly increase in difficulty, and the test is similar to State and Chapter MATHCOUNTS Sprints from recent years.
